The Hidden Cost of Notifications

Every buzz and banner pulls attention away from the task at hand. Researchers have spent years measuring how long it takes to get back on track after an interruption, and the answer is longer than most people think. In one study, office workers needed more than twenty minutes to return to their original task.

That number matters because the average knowledge worker receives dozens of notifications every hour. Even when people ignore them, the mere presence of a phone on the desk measurably reduces working memory capacity, according to experiments run at several universities over the past decade.
